Accessibility Statement — Harmony Studio

Statement updated: 17 November 2026 · Last accessibility check: 17 November 2026

1. Our commitment

Harmony Studio wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website independently and comfortably, and works to make it accessible.

2. Accessibility level

The Website was built in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard 5568 (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) at level AA. Some parts of the Website may not yet be fully accessible — see "Known limitations" below.

3. Adjustments made

  • Semantic structure, ordered headings and page landmarks for screen-reader navigation.
  • Full keyboard navigation, a logical focus order, a visible focus indicator and a "skip to content" link.
  • Semantic HTML and ARIA where needed, so that assistive technologies, including screen readers, can be used.
  • Text alternatives for informative images; decorative images marked so they are not read out.
  • Colour contrast as required, and information is not conveyed by colour alone.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content or function; responsive layout for phone, tablet and desktop.
  • Correct right-to-left (RTL) layout where applicable, and declared page language.
  • Respect for the user's reduced-motion preference.

4. Accessibility toolbar

The Website includes an accessibility toolbar (the accessibility icon at the corner of the screen) that lets you change text size, contrast (high contrast and inverted colours), greyscale, highlight links, use a readable font, and reset your settings. Your choices are saved in your browser. The toolbar is a supplementary aid only and does not replace making the Website itself accessible.

5. Environments tested

The Website was tested in: the Chrome browser, checked by hand and with automated checks at desktop and phone widths, in light and dark mode, in Hebrew, Arabic and English.

6. Known limitations

No screen-reader test and no full keyboard test have been carried out. Text over photographs and videos was checked by eye, not by measurement. The site has scroll animations and videos; the animations are reduced when your device is set to reduce motion. The legal pages exist in Hebrew and English and not yet in Arabic. Issues we did not find may exist, and we will gladly fix any issue reported to us.

Content originating from third parties that we do not edit or produce — such as maps, videos and payment interfaces, where present on the Website — may not be fully accessible. Where possible we will make it accessible or provide an alternative.

7. Alternatives and accessible service

If you have difficulty using the Website, we will gladly provide the information or service in an accessible alternative way — by phone, email or in person, as needed.

8. Accessibility enquiries

You may notify us of missing accessibility adjustments, request that content or a service be made accessible, or send a demand under Regulation 35A(d)(1):

  • Accessibility contact: Customer service
  • Phone: 050-281-7131

We will handle your enquiry as soon as possible and in any event within 60 days of receiving it. Until the fix is completed we will offer an alternative adjustment where possible. To help us, please include the page address, the problem, and the assistive technology you used.

9. Updates

This statement was updated on 17 November 2026. We update it when changes affecting accessibility are made to the Website.
