Privacy Policy — Harmony Studio

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

The controller of your personal information (the "database controller") is Harmony Studio ("we").

For privacy requests, including access, correction or deletion: Tel 050-281-7131.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to personal information we collect through Harmony Studio (the "Website"): when you browse, or use the services shown on it. "Personal information" means data relating to an identified or identifiable person, including online identifiers (such as cookie IDs or IP addresses) and location data.

The policy also covers information you give us on your own initiative through the contact channels published on the Website (for example by phone or message); we use it only to answer your enquiry and manage the relationship. Wording in one gender applies to all genders.

We may also use information to (a) secure the Website and prevent fraud or misuse; (b) comply with legal obligations; (c) protect our rights or defend legal claims; and (d) produce aggregated statistics that do not identify you. 6. Transfers outside Israel

Some of our service providers operate or store data outside Israel. Transfers are made in accordance with applicable law, and we require reasonable security and confidentiality measures from providers.

7. Cookies and tracking technologies

The Website does not use non-essential cookies.

The Website stores in your browser (local storage) only choices you made, so you do not have to choose again: harmony-lang — remembering the display language you chose (Hebrew, Arabic or English); site-theme — remembering the display mode you chose (light or dark); harmony-a11y — remembering the settings you chose in the accessibility toolbar (text size, contrast, etc.). This information stays on your device, does not identify you, and is not sent to us or to any third party. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings, and you will then need to choose again.

8. Promotional messages

We do not send promotional messages.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), restricted access and providers bound by security commitments. No system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee immunity from intrusion or failure. If a material security incident occurs we will act as the law requires, including notifying the authority and affected people where required.

11. Your rights

  • Access the personal information we hold about you (section 13 of the Protection of Privacy Law).
  • Correction or deletion of information that is incorrect, incomplete, unclear or outdated (section 14). Deletion is subject to record-keeping duties under law.

To exercise a right, contact us via the contact details published on the Website. We may ask for details to verify your identity. We will respond within the period the law prescribes.

12. Children

The Website is not intended for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If we learn that we have, or if a parent or guardian asks us to, we will delete it.

13. Links to other websites

The Website may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published with its date and applies from publication. For a material change in how we use information already provided, we will notify you prominently and, where needed, seek your consent again.

15. Questions and complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, contact us again via the contact details published on the Website; you may also contact the Privacy Protection Authority at the Israeli Ministry of Justice.
